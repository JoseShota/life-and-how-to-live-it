## 1. The idea in one paragraph

You run seven activities and you don't have a steady job. That's a lot of plates. The danger isn't that you'll fail at one of them. It's that your hours spread across all seven, none of them compounds, and your money never gets beyond "survive the month". **Life and How to Live It** turns your life into a game with three scoreboards:

| Scoreboard | Question it answers | Main metric |
|---|---|---|
| ⚔️ **Quests** (work) | Am I moving the right things forward? | Deep-work hours/day, quests done, hours per area vs target |
| 🛡️ **Routine** (body/mind) | Am I the person who can sustain this? | Perfect Days, streaks (wake, sleep, exercise) |
| 💰 **Treasury** (wealth) | Is my net worth going up, and *where* does my money come from? | Net worth, savings rate, **real MXN per hour by activity** |

XP and coins connect all three: every good action pays you right away, and you spend coins on real rewards.

## 2. Your seven activities — portfolio view (an economist's lens)

Treat your activities like an investment portfolio. Each one gets a **role**, a **weekly hour budget**, and a **kill/scale rule**.

| # | Activity | Role | Hrs/wk (start) | What "winning" looks like | Monetization path |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🎓 Teaching Intro to Economics @ CIDE | **Base income + prestige** | 8 | Classes ready 48 h ahead, grading never late | Turn lectures into assets (course, newsletter, YouTube clips) |
| 2 | 🧵 Weaving Memories (web, media, admin) | **Base income + portfolio** | 12 | Deliverables on time, 1 automation shipped per month | Raise scope/rate with documented automations |
| 3 | 💻 Freelance web dev | **Cash engine** | 10 | 1 packaged offer, a running pipeline | Fixed-price packages → monthly retainers |
| 4 | 🤖 AI automation | **Asymmetric bet (highest upside)** | 7 | Ship real automations, publish case studies | "AI automation for SMEs/academics" → productized service → product |
| 5 | 🎬 Video editing (hobby) | **Recharge + content skill** | 3 | 1 short per week | Later: content for #4 and #7 |
| 6 | ✍️ Writing (hobby) | **Recharge + thinking** | 3 | 300 words/day streak | Later: newsletter = audience = leverage |
| 7 | 🌐 Personal webpage | **Distribution hub** | 2 | Clear offer + case studies + contact | Inbound leads for #3 and #4 |
| 8 | 💼 Wealth & life admin | **Protection** | 2 | Every peso logged; cards paid in full | — |

**The strategic insight:** #3, #4 and #7 are really **one business**: *"Economist who builds AI automations and websites."* An economist who can automate with AI is rare and pricey. A generic web freelancer is a commodity. Point all your marketing at that combined identity. #1 (CIDE) gives you credibility. #5 and #6 give you the content skills that bring people in.

**Rules for the portfolio (the app enforces these with data):**
1. **The Wealth Engine table** (Money → "where your hours pay most") ranks activities by **real MXN per hour** over 90 days (30 and 365 also available): what an activity leaves you after taxes and its own costs, divided by *all* the hours it takes (focus sessions plus commuting, admin, sales and class time). Every month, move 2–3 weekly hours from the lowest-paying monetizable activity to the highest.
2. **Kill rule:** if a monetizable activity's real rate stays below your floor rate for 3 months and teaches you nothing new, shrink it. The floor comes from your real numbers: monthly spending ÷ (1 − the savings % you pay yourself first) ÷ the paid hours you actually work in a month. It moves as they change (it can be set by hand in the Wealth Engine).
3. **Scale rule:** if an activity's real rate is ≥ 2× your average real rate, give it more hours *and* raise its price.

## 3. The routine — engineered, not wished for

### Sleep math (it's the foundation)
- Wake **05:45** + sleep **≥ 7 h 30 m** ⇒ asleep by **22:15 at the latest**.
- Plan: **in bed 21:45 → lights out 22:00** (gives a 15-min buffer to fall asleep → ~7 h 45 m).
- Screens off at **21:15**. The phone charges outside the bedroom. Use a real alarm clock.
- Same wake time **7 days a week**, weekends included. Consistency matters more than intensity.

Work starts at **08:45** (changed 2026-11-09 from 08:00; set in Control → "Work starts at"). The extra 45 minutes go to a longer exercise, breakfast and intel; the whole work day moves 45 minutes later, and the evening's free time absorbs it. Wake-up, the shutdown and bedtime don't move.

Targets on those days: **4 h done, 5 h full**. The weekly 30 h stays. The hours away (leave → home) count automatically as non-focus hours for the real MXN/h.

### Anti-fragility rules (this is why most routines fail, and how yours won't)
1. **Never miss twice.** One missed day is noise. Two in a row is the start of a new habit. The app shows streaks, and in Phase 4 it adds a "streak shield".
2. **Minimum viable version.** On bad days: 10 push-ups, 5-minute tidy, 1 newsletter, 1 hour of work. It still counts, so the chain stays alive.
3. **Intel has a hard cap.** Information is not progress. The intel block (60 minutes with work at 08:45), then close it. (Phase 3: the AI summarizes your newsletters so the block covers everything.)
4. **Sunday Review (30 m):** read this plan, check the Archive, adjust weekly hour targets, plan the week's quests, export a backup.

## 4. The wealth plan — "getting rich, no joke"

The equation an economist already knows:

> **Wealth = (Income − Spending) × Time × Return**

Most people only work on *return* (investing tips). For you, **income is by far the biggest lever**, then the savings rate, then time. Here's the order of operations:

### Level 1 — Stop the bleeding (Months 0–1)
- [ ] Log **every** transaction for 30 days (achievement: *Bookkeeper*). You can't optimize what you don't measure.
- [ ] Enter each Banorte card's **limit, cut-off date (fecha de corte) and pay-by date (fecha límite de pago)** in the app.
- [ ] **Pay every card in full every month** (pay the *"pago para no generar intereses"* amount). Mexican credit card interest (CAT) is extremely high, often far above anything you could earn by investing. Revolving debt is the #1 enemy. If you carry debt now, use the **avalanche** method: pay minimums on everything and throw all extra money at the card with the highest rate.
- [ ] Set up **automatic payment (domiciliación)** of the full statement balance from your debit card, if Banorte allows it for your cards.
- [ ] Keep utilization **< 30 %** of each limit (the app warns you).
- [ ] **Use the different cut-off dates as a tool:** for a big planned purchase, buy right *after* a card's cut-off date. That gives you the longest interest-free window (~45–50 days). Only do this if you'll pay it in full.

### Level 2 — Build the shield (Months 1–6)
- [ ] **Emergency fund #1:** one month of expenses.
- [ ] **Emergency fund #2:** **six months**, because your income is irregular. Keep it liquid and safe, e.g. short-term Mexican government bonds (CETES via *cetesdirecto*) or a high-yield account. Compare current rates before choosing.
- [ ] **Pay-yourself-first split.** Every time money comes in, move it right away:

| Bucket | % (start) | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Operating (living costs) | 50 % | Banorte debit |
| Tax reserve | exact, per invoice | Separate savings account (*Apartado impuestos*). In RESICO the app computes it from each CFDI: ISR at the month's rate (1–2.5 %) minus what the client withheld, plus the IVA you'll pay over. The monthly declaration is due on the 17th |
| Shield → then investments | 10 % to start, +2 points whenever a month beats your 3-month median income (Save More Tomorrow), up to 20 %+ | Emergency fund until 3 months, then 25 % to investments; at 6 months, all to investments |
| Skills (courses, tools, AI credits) | 10 % | Debit |
| Fun (guilt-free) | 5 % | Cash / debit. This pairs with the app's coin shop |

### Level 3 — Build the income engine (Months 1–12) ← **the real game**
Saving only gets you so far. Getting rich comes from **raising your hourly value** and then **separating income from hours**.

1. **Position:** *"Economist who builds AI automations and websites for businesses and academics."* Put it on your webpage, LinkedIn, and your signature.
2. **Productize:** sell 3 fixed-price packages instead of open-ended hourly work:
   - *Quick Win* — one automation (e.g. invoices → spreadsheet → report), fixed price, delivered in 1 week.
   - *Website + Automations* — website plus 2 automations.
   - *Retainer* — monthly maintenance, new automations and reporting. **Retainers are the key to steady income without a steady job.**
3. **Pipeline discipline:** the app's **Opportunity Pipeline** tracks every lead with its monthly value × probability. Target: **5 new conversations per week**. The expected pipeline value is your leading indicator. Income is the lagging one.
4. **Proof:** every automation you build becomes a **case study** on your website (that's Area #7 doing its job).
5. **Leverage (Months 6–12+):** turn repeated work into assets: templates, a small course ("AI automation for economists/analysts"), a newsletter. CIDE teaching and writing feed this.
6. **Price increases:** raise prices every time you're booked > 80 %, and at least every 6 months.

### Level 4 — Compound (two steps, approved 2026-11-06)
- [ ] **Starter, at a 3-month shield:** 25 % of the savings bucket goes **automatically every month** to diversified, low-cost, long-term instruments (e.g. broad index ETFs through a regulated Mexican broker). The rest keeps filling the shield. Starting small builds the investing habit early.
- [ ] **Full, at a 6-month shield** (and 3 statements in a row paid in full): the whole savings bucket goes to investments.
- [ ] If the shield is spent in an emergency, investing pauses until it's refilled. The level you reached stays yours.
- [ ] **Tax note:** voluntary **AFORE** contributions and a **PPR** are deductible (LISR art. 151 fr. V) only outside RESICO. Under RESICO there are no personal deductions, so choose vehicles for return and liquidity, not for the deduction.
- [ ] Don't pick stocks with money you need. Don't invest money that belongs in the shield.

**What the numbers say** (7 % annual *nominal*, monthly contributions, illustrative only; in today's pesos at about 3.7 % inflation, 10k/month for 10 years ≈ 1.41 M, 25k ≈ 3.53 M, 40k ≈ 5.64 M):

| Invest per month | 10 years | 15 years |
|---|---|---|
| 10,000 MXN | ≈ 1.73 M | — |
| 15,000 MXN | ≈ 2.60 M | — |
| 25,000 MXN | ≈ 4.33 M | ≈ 7.92 M |
| 40,000 MXN | ≈ 6.92 M | — |

The jump from 10k to 40k a month comes **entirely from the income engine (Level 3)**. That's why your deep work block 1 is always *the money quest*.

### Milestones (wired in as Wealth Goals in the app)
🛡️ 1-month shield → 💳 0 revolving debt → 🏰 6-month shield → 🚀 1,000,000 MXN invested → 🏛️ 10 M (later)

In the app these are **wealth levels** (Treasury → The Road): 1 Stop the bleeding · 2 Shield I (1 month) · 3 Compound starter (3-month shield) · 4 Fortress (6-month shield, full investing) · 5 First million invested. Shield months are measured against your real median monthly spending.

## 5. The two December opportunities — decision framework

You're waiting on two opportunities in December. Don't just wait. **Prepare for all four outcomes now**, so December is a decision, not a crisis.

### A. Before December (now → Dec 1)
- [ ] Add both to the **Opportunity Pipeline** with: expected monthly value, probability, expected date, next action.
- [ ] Set a follow-up date for each, and do it.
- [ ] Write down your **walk-away number** (the minimum monthly pay + conditions that make it worth taking).
- [ ] Keep building the freelance/AI pipeline anyway. Never let "waiting" eat your hours.

### B. Scenario plan

| Scenario | Action |
|---|---|
| ✅✅ Both offered | Score them with the matrix below. Negotiate one against the other. Ask if one can be part-time or a consultancy |
| ✅❌ One offered | Score it. If it beats your walk-away number, take it and **keep the AI/freelance engine at a reduced but non-zero budget** (e.g. 5–7 h/week) |
| ❌❌ Neither | No panic. You've already built the pipeline. Run a 6-week "Freelance Sprint": 10 conversations/week, 1 public case study/week |
| ⏳ Delayed | Ask for a decision date. Pipeline keeps running |

### C. Scoring matrix (score each 1–5, multiply by weight)

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Guaranteed monthly income (stability) | 30 % |
| Upside / growth in pay over 2 years | 20 % |
| Builds AI + economics positioning | 20 % |
| Time flexibility (protects the income engine) | 15 % |
| Network / prestige | 10 % |
| Commute / stress / health cost (inverse) | 5 % |

A steady job **doesn't kill the plan, it funds it**. The stable salary fills the shield fast, and the engine keeps running on a smaller budget.
