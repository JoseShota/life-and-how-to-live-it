## Decisions: the agent's recommendation, to confirm with the user in one line
The user asked for the agent's opinion on these two points. The recommendations below were given in chat; **confirm before acting**, because the first one edits `00-MASTER-PLAN.md`.

1. **The boss fight's 60 % should be Good-or-Perfect days, not Perfect Days.**
   - `00-MASTER-PLAN.md` §6 says "30-day Perfect-Day-rate ≥ 60 %". But Economy B (D-1 to D-3) made a Perfect Day a rare stretch on purpose: 2 to 6 % of days in `scripts/eval-sim.ts`. So that boss would be unwinnable.
   - The evaluation's calibrated target is **Good-or-Perfect days ≥ 60 %** (63 % in the realistic scenario, 51 % with a bad week a month), which is what the 2026-11-08 handoff already uses.
   - Recommendation: change that one line in §6 to "30-day Good-or-Perfect rate ≥ 60 %" once the user says yes. Change nothing else in the master plan.
2. **Leave two things out of E-9:**
   - **The coin wager on the weekly scorecard.** It is an XP-economy change, and a loss-framed bet in a new system's first weeks risks a bad week feeling like a double loss. Keep it as a Season 2 experiment, next to Option C.
   - **A new "month's plan" screen.** It would duplicate the weekly review and Treasury. The first-of-month banner links to screens that already exist; build a monthly screen only if the banner proves too thin.
